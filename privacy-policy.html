Privacy Policy

How Cosyklick collects, uses and protects information when you interact with our website and services.

Last updated: August 9, 2026

1. Overview

Cosyklick respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how Cosyklick may collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, request our services or otherwise interact with us.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

You may provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, company name, business information, marketing requirements and other information you choose to submit.

Information Collected Automatically

When you use our website, technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referral source and website interaction data may be collected automatically.

3. How We Use Information

  • Respond to enquiries and requests.
  • Provide and improve our services.
  • Prepare proposals and marketing recommendations.
  • Communicate with clients and prospective clients.
  • Measure website and campaign performance.
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse or fraud.
  • Meet applicable legal and contractual obligations.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cosyklick may use cookies, analytics tools, pixels and similar technologies to understand website traffic, remember preferences, improve functionality and measure marketing performance. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

5. Marketing Communications

If you provide your contact information, we may contact you regarding your enquiry or our services. Where applicable, promotional communications will follow relevant legal requirements and provide appropriate opt-out options.

6. Sharing Information

Cosyklick does not intend to sell or rent your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers, technology providers, analytics providers, advertising platforms and professional advisers when reasonably necessary to operate our business and provide services.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect our rights, prevent fraud or misuse, or in connection with a business transaction.

7. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website or services may use or link to third-party advertising, analytics, communication, social media, hosting and technology services. These services have their own policies, and Cosyklick is not responsible for practices outside our control.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for providing services, maintaining records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements and complying with legal obligations.

10. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other action concerning your personal information.

To make a request, please contact Cosyklick through the Contact page .

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed toward children, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children through our website.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements or business practices. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
